# 🏥 AyurCard – The Future of Healthcare

An AI-powered Healthcare Management System designed to centralize patient medical records, streamline doctor-patient interactions, and assist healthcare professionals through Machine Learning-based disease prediction.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

AyurCard is an intelligent healthcare platform that provides secure access to patient medical records through a unique AyurCard ID. The system enables patients, doctors, and administrators to perform their respective operations efficiently while leveraging Artificial Intelligence for chronic and acute disease prediction.

## 🔄 System Workflow

1. Patient registers and logs in.
2. Doctor registers and logs in.
3. Patient books an appointment.
4. Doctor views appointments.
5. Doctor fetches patient medical history using the AyurCard ID.
6. Doctor enters current symptoms.
7. AI predicts acute and chronic diseases.
8. Doctor records diagnosis and prescription.
9. Medical history is updated.
10. Admin manages the complete healthcare system.
